1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They're widely used to make websites work, work more efficiently, and to provide information to the site owner.

2. What this website currently uses

As published, this website uses only strictly necessary local storage — specifically, a single browser localStorage entry that remembers whether you've dismissed the cookie notice banner, so it isn't shown to you again. No advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking cookies are set by this template out of the box.

  • Name: ml-cookie-consent
  • Purpose: Remembers your cookie notice preference
  • Duration: Persists until you clear your browser storage
  • Type: Strictly necessary (no consent required under UK PECR)

3. If analytics or other tools are added

If this website is later configured to use analytics (such as Google Analytics), advertising cookies, or embedded third-party content (such as a video widget that sets its own cookies), those tools would typically require your consent before being activated, and this policy — along with the cookie banner — should be updated to reflect them individually, including their name, purpose, and duration.

4. How to control cookies

Most browsers let you see, manage, and delete cookies through their settings. You can also use our on-site cookie banner to choose "Essential only" if it's still showing, or clear your browser's local storage to reset your preference and see the banner again.

5. Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@lexentlawchamber.example. See also our Privacy Policy for how we handle personal data more broadly.
