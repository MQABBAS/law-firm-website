Our Story

Built on trust, guided by clarity

Lexent Law Chamber was founded on a simple idea: people navigating an immigration or asylum matter deserve advice that's honest, easy to understand, and genuinely focused on their outcome — not just their case file. Immigration law changes often, and the stakes for our clients are rarely small, so we chose to work in this one area and do it properly, rather than spread ourselves across unrelated fields of law.

Today, our advisers support individuals, families and sponsoring employers through visa applications, asylum claims and appeals, combining thorough up-to-date knowledge with a personal, approachable service. Whatever brings you to us, we start by listening.

Regulation

Regulated immigration advice you can rely on

Giving immigration advice in the UK is a regulated activity. Lexent Law Chamber operates within the Immigration Advice Authority (IAA) framework, and our advisers hold (or are working towards) the relevant level of accreditation for the matters they handle. Full registration numbers and accreditation levels for each adviser are available on request and will be published here once confirmed.
