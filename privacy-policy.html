Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal data.

1. Who we are

Lexent Law Chamber ("we", "us", "our") is the data controller for personal data collected through this website. Our contact details are set out in the "How to contact us" section below.

2. What information we collect

We may collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Contact details you provide via our booking form, email, phone, or WhatsApp — name, email address, phone number, and the content of your enquiry.
  • Case-related information you share with us if you become a client, which will typically include sensitive categories of data given the nature of immigration and asylum matters (for example, details relevant to a protection claim).
  • Technical information such as browser type and general usage data, if analytics tools are added to this site.

3. How we use your information

We use personal data to respond to enquiries, provide immigration and asylum advice to clients, comply with our legal and regulatory obligations, and maintain records as required by professional conduct rules. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

4. Our legal basis for processing

Depending on the context, we rely on: your explicit consent (particularly for sensitive data connected to a protection or asylum claim), the steps necessary to enter into or perform a contract with you as a client, our legal obligations (such as anti-money laundering and identity checks), and our legitimate interests in running and improving our practice.

5. Sharing your information

We may share personal data with third parties who support our practice — such as IT and case management providers, barristers or other professionals instructed on your case, interpreters, the Home Office and relevant tribunals where required for your matter, and regulators or courts where required by law. We require any third party handling data on our behalf to keep it secure and use it only for the purposes we specify.

6. How long we keep your data

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal and regulatory retention requirements (including professional conduct rules on file retention), and establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Enquiry data from people who don't become clients is generally retained for a shorter period.

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you generally have the right to access, correct, or request erasure of your personal data, to restrict or object to certain processing, and to data portability. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you believe your data has been mishandled.

8. Cookies

This website uses only essential cookies/local storage needed for the site to function (for example, remembering your cookie preference). See our Cookie Policy for full details.

9. Security

Given the sensitive nature of immigration and asylum matters, we take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

11. How to contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at info@lexentlawchamber.com or +44 20 7946 0958.
