The Brief

A trust-sensitive site for a
regulated legal practice

Lexent Law Chamber needed a website that could do two things at once: read as immediately credible to people navigating one of the most stressful processes in their lives, and perform seriously as an acquisition channel — organic search, Google Ads, and word of mouth all needed to land somewhere polished. Every design and content decision was made with that tension in mind.

Trust & Compliance First

Regulator status, complaints procedure, privacy/cookie/terms pages, and a disclaimer footer built in from day one — not bolted on later.

SEO Built on Real Research

Competitive positioning grounded in the Solicitors Regulation Authority's own published thematic review and Chambers/Legal 500 market data — not invented claims.

Mobile-First, Always

Hero layout, navigation and CTAs re-ordered specifically for phone visitors — where most enquiries for a practice like this actually originate.

What Was Built

Every detail, custom-built

No page builder, no theme. Every line of HTML, CSS and JavaScript was hand-written to this client's brief.

Custom Animated Branding

A hand-coded scales-of-justice icon that gently sways beside the wordmark, a gold-shimmer text treatment, and a typewriter-effect headline that cycles through persuasive variants — all built from scratch in vanilla JS, with zero dependencies.

Real, Cited SEO Strategy

Structured data (LegalService + FAQPage schema), a full XML sitemap, and on-page copy shaped by genuine market research into the top UK immigration firms — not fabricated reviews or invented statistics.

Custom Domain Migration

Migrated the entire site from a GitHub Pages subdomain to the client's own registered domain — every canonical URL, Open Graph tag, and structured data reference updated in one clean pass.

Ad-Platform Ready

Google Ads conversion tracking installed site-wide, plus ready-to-use Responsive Search Ad headlines, descriptions and sitelinks — the client could launch a campaign the same day.

Accessible by Default

Every animation on the site — the typewriter, the scroll-reveal system, the ambient glows — checks prefers-reduced-motion and disables itself for visitors who need that.

Genuinely Useful Content

A free library of in-depth, plain-English immigration & asylum guides — real answers to real questions, not thin SEO filler.

How It Came Together

Iterative, hands-on delivery

Brand & Rebuild

Full rebrand and rebuild of the site's identity, navy-and-gold visual system, and every core page from the ground up.

Content Library

Ten in-depth immigration & asylum guides written and published, covering the questions clients actually search for.

Animation & Polish

Custom logo animation, typewriter headline, scroll-reveal system and hover interactions layered on across every page.

Go-Live

Custom domain connected, Google Ads tag installed, sitemap finalised, and the site handed over fully live and ready for its first campaign.
